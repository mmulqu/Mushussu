## BASELINE VOICE (from Chapter 6)

### Stylistic Characteristics:
- **POV**: Deep third-person limited, anchored in character perspective
- **Prose Style**: Literary, economical, McCarthy-esque restraint
- **Sentence Structure**: Mix of fragments and longer sentences, often paratactic (coordinate clauses without subordination)
- **Imagery**: Concrete, sensory, grounded in physical detail
- **Tone**: Elegiac, foreboding, atmospheric
- **Dialogue**: Sparse, naturalistic, loaded with subtext
- **Internal Thoughts**: Italicized, stream-of-consciousness fragments
- **Metaphor**: Organic, drawn from natural world, never precious or overworked

### Example Passages (Ch6):
```
"James had first heard the wail as he exited the hotel to chop more firewood forage mushrooms and a subconscious reminiscence skipped over his mind like a stone on water."

"Under her long black hair disarrayed were her shining full eyes. Eyes open but not seeing their surroundings, but rather seeing through them, he conjectured."

"The fires of hell with him. Journey to a dead planet as we kill this one."
```

### Key Elements:
1. **Restraint**: Emotion implied through action/image, not stated directly
2. **Rhythm**: Varied sentence length creates breathing, musical quality
3. **Specificity**: Concrete details (pancakes, axe, beech tree) anchor abstract moments
4. **Ambiguity**: Leaves room for reader interpretation
5. **Greek Tragedy Structure**: Fate/doom woven subtly through imagery
