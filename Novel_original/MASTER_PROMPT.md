## 📖 NOVEL IDENTITY

### Title (Working)
**Persephone** (or variants exploring mythological resonance)

### Genre & Tone
- **Climate Dystopian Literary Fiction**
- **Psychological Thriller with Philosophical Undertones**
- Atmospheric, elegiac, dense with allusion
- Balances intimate character study with sweeping ecological/metaphysical themes

### Core Premise
An ornithologist grieving the loss of her daughter undergoes AI implant surgery to regulate her emotions. As she pursues what may be the last breeding pair of Golden-winged Warblers in rural Maine, her malfunctioning implant blurs the boundaries between memory, madness, and transcendence—revealing layers of Greek tragedy, accelerationist philosophy, and the inexorable collapse of both human civilization and the natural world.

## 🎭 CHARACTERS

### Persephone (Protagonist)
**Role**: Ornithologist, mother, widow of her former self

**Physical**: Black hair, worn hiking boots, binoculars always at hand

**Background**:
- Ph.D. in ornithology (Golden-winged Warbler specialist)
- Former doctoral work in Quebec—idyllic summers studying breeding warblers
- Married to Mike (aerospace engineer working on Ares mission)
- Mother to Iphy (Iphigenia)—deceased daughter whose death drives the narrative

**Psychological Profile**:
- Profound grief and rage following Iphy's death
- Blames Mike for the "incident" (nature of incident kept ambiguous early on)
- Resistant to AI augmentation technology ("Augs") but desperate for relief
- Volunteers for dawn bird collision surveys—acts of ecological witness and penance
- Deep connection to birds as symbols of migration, extinction, and futility

**Arc**:
- Begins in Miami: surveying dead migratory birds, preparing for AI surgery
- Undergoes "Omphalic" implant surgery (brownout during procedure = malfunction seed)
- Travels to Maine to find reported Golden-winged Warbler pair
- Implant malfunction escalates—visions, memory intrusions, emotional dysregulation
- Encounters mysterious figures in woods (witches? Reality? Hallucination?)
- Journey becomes psychopomp narrative—descent into underworld of grief/madness

**Key Relationships**:
- **Mike (ex-husband)**: Estranged, blamed for Iphy's death, suffers psychotic break, murders James
- **Iphy (daughter)**: Deceased; haunts narrative through memory and guilt
- **Larry (supervisor)**: Alerts her to Maine GWWA sighting
- **James (cult member)**: Groomed her, drugged her, murdered by Mike in Ch12
- **Charlie (gas station attendant)**: Charon figure, ferries her into Maine's underworld

- Habitat: shrubby wetlands adjacent to mature forests

## ✍️ WRITING STYLE

### Narrative Voice
**Third-Person Limited** (Persephone's POV dominates)
- Close psychic distance—stream of consciousness at times
- **Exception**: Prologue (second-person bird POV)

### Prose Characteristics
**Lyrical & Dense**:
- Long, flowing sentences with embedded clauses
- Rich sensory detail (visual, auditory, tactile)
- Layered allusions (Greek myth, literature, ecology, philosophy)

**Joycean Influence**:
- Stream-of-consciousness passages
- Lists (Joycean catalogues of stimuli, horrors, beauties)
- Wordplay and neologisms ("cybervolving," "teleoplexy")
- **Note in Ch5**: "Proteus chapter (Ulysses)"—suggests intentional Joyce homage

**Atmospheric**:
- Emphasis on weather, light, shadow, sound
- Nature descriptions oscillate between beautiful and menacing
- "Valhalla Rising" influence: eerie, lurking, oppressive darkness

**Philosophical**:
- Intrusions of Persephone's ruminations on extinction, time, futility
- Questions without answers
- Weighs individual suffering against cosmic indifference

### Sentence Examples (Stylistic Benchmarks)
> "The sinking of Apollo's golden chariot sank into the west signalled to the anxious crowds to ascend."

> "A hazy seawind lifted the leaves gently, a plea to awaken. Gloomy silhouettes stirred not through the still stands of trees, each a shadow in the dark."

> "She fell into the black pool through her waves. Whipping up, she looked down to a sea of eyes, a million pupils staring back at her through their black viscous sclera."

> "The white lines extended forever into the distance straight as a monolith's shadow on the solstice."

### POV Experiments
**Prologue (Second-Person Bird POV)**:
- "You broke the green veil to an unfiltered precipice."
- Immerses reader in migratory instinct, predator-prey terror
- Establishes nature as nihilistic, primal

- Persephone's unresolved mourning for Iphy

- Trauma loops (yellow birds, memories, implant visions)

**2. Extinction & Futility**
- Golden-winged Warbler as doomed species
- Climate collapse as slow catastrophe

**3. Technology & Humanity**
- AI implants as Faustian bargain
- Loss of autonomy, personhood, authenticity
- "Buggers" as cautionary figures

**4. Migration & Instinct**
- Birds compelled by ancient pathways

- "Impossibility of stopping escape"
- Question: Are we free, or driven by forces beyond comprehension?

**5. Nature's Indifference**

- Vernal pool as Lovecraftian horror/womb

**6. Descent & Transformation**
- Persephone's underworld journey
- Madness as gateway to truth?
- Pan's Hour: encounter with primal chaos
- Question: What will she become?
